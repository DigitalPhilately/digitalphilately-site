Stamp collecting in the digital age

Digital philately is the use of digital technology to collect, identify, study, catalogue and exhibit postage stamps and postal history. It also covers newer forms of collecting, including digital-only stamps, crypto stamps and NFTs.

It is not one technology. It is everything from a barcode scanned on a phone to a searchable archive, an online exhibition or a stamp that exists only on a blockchain.

The six areas of digital philately

Digital identification

Scanning stamps, recognising designs and decoding barcodes. Software can now tell you which stamp you are holding, sometimes faster than a catalogue can.

From Modern GB

  • Stamp Decoder — scan a Royal Mail barcoded stamp and see, in plain collector terms, exactly which stamp it is

Digital collections

Virtual albums, collection databases and records of individual stamps, so a collection can be searched, valued and passed on.

Philatelic research

Online catalogues, searchable databases and digitised archives. Research that once needed a society library can now start from a phone.

From Modern GB

  • Barcoded Stamps — a free reference catalogue of Royal Mail barcoded definitives and Christmas stamps

Digital exhibiting

Virtual exhibitions, online displays and presentations that let a collection reach people who will never see the album.

Digital stamps

Stamps issued in digital form, including crypto stamps and NFT stamps. The Universal Postal Union has published a taxonomy for these products and runs a Digital Philately Academy.

Connected collecting

Online communities, shared research and digital marketplaces: the way collectors now find each other, swap knowledge and buy stamps.

Recognised across the hobby

The Universal Postal Union has published a formal taxonomy of digital philatelic products and runs a Digital Philately Academy, with a particular focus on crypto stamps, NFTs and blockchain. Collectors, societies and researchers have long used digitisation, computer-assisted research and electronic exhibitions too. Digital philately covers all of these.

Others working in this field

  • Digital Philately: The Virtual Frontier, writing about digital exhibiting, electronic literature and computer-assisted research since 2008.
  • The UPU WADP Digital Philately Academy, a knowledge hub for postal operators on crypto stamps, NFTs and blockchain.
